The Data

Real-world ground truth

The UIEB (Underwater Image Enhancement Benchmark) contains 890 raw underwater photographs shot in open water, each paired with a reference image processed by domain experts. This gives the GAN real ground truth rather than synthetic degradation — the model learns to invert actual underwater physics, not simulated noise.

U-Net Architecture

8-level encoder–decoder with skip connections

Skip connections between each encoder and decoder level allow high-frequency spatial detail to bypass the bottleneck. This is critical for image restoration: the encoder captures what needs to change (color cast, haze), while the skip connections preserve what should stay (edges, textures, structure).

Discriminator

PatchGAN Classifier

The discriminator sees both the raw input and the generated output as a 6-channel concatenated tensor. Rather than classifying the entire image as real or fake, it classifies overlapping 70×70 patches independently.

This forces the generator to produce realistic local textures at every location in the image — penalizing artifacts at the patch scale rather than relying on global statistics alone.

Optimization

Loss functions

L_adv = BCE(D(x, G(x)), 1)

Adversarial Loss

Generator tries to fool the discriminator into classifying its output as real.

L_pix = λ · ‖G(x) − y‖₁

Pixel Loss (λ = 100)

L1 distance to ground truth. High λ ensures structural fidelity dominates over texture.

L_G = L_adv + L_pix

Total Generator Loss

Combined loss drives both perceptual realism and pixel-level accuracy.
